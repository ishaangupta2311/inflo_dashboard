FAQ

Questions, answered.

Want to talk strategy before you buy?

Do you guarantee coverage?
Our single feature packages are coverage-guaranteed. Larger campaigns target a range of features; we’ve hit our coverage targets on 96% of campaigns to date.
Which publications can you reach?
From global tier-1 outlets to high-authority niche and trade media. We match publications to your audience and sector for relevance, not just vanity.
How long does a campaign take?
First coverage typically lands within 21 days. Full campaigns run over 4–6 weeks to maximise pickup.
Will the links be dofollow?
Many features include dofollow links, but editorial policies vary by outlet. Even nofollow tier-1 coverage carries strong brand and trust signals — and often drives referral traffic for years.
